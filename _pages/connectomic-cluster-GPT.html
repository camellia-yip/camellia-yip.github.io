01 / Overview

From familiar bundles to finer connections

White matter connects distributed regions of the brain. Major anatomical bundles are well studied, while smaller pathways and the fine structure within those bundles remain harder to characterize.

We construct a whole-brain fiber-cluster resource from high-resolution diffusion MRI. Streamlines are grouped using their geometry and the regions they connect, allowing established bundles and finer subdivisions to be examined in the same atlas.

  • Fine-grained organization. Two atlas versions use 7 or 17 cortical functional networks, each combined with nine subcortical regions.
  • Complementary reconstructions. Probabilistic and deterministic tractography are combined before clustering.
  • Population and individual views. Corresponding clusters are examined at the group level and within individual participants.

02 / Method

From diffusion MRI to a fiber-cluster atlas

The pipeline combines multiple tractography methods, a compact representation of streamline geometry, and filtering at both population and individual levels.

  1. 01

    Reconstruct

    Estimate multi-tissue fiber orientation distributions and generate streamlines with iFOD1, iFOD2 and SD_STREAM. Align the reconstructions to MNI152 space.

  2. 02

    Represent & cluster

    Group streamlines by their connected regions. Encode their positions and shapes with cosine-series coefficients, then apply k-means clustering.

  3. 03

    Filter & compare

    Remove outlying streamlines at group and individual levels. Compare the resulting clusters across participants and with the population atlas.

03 / Results

What the finer scale reveals

The atlas contains 33,256 clusters under the 7-network scheme and 65,184 under the 17-network scheme, spanning deep and superficial white matter.

Interpreting the atlas

Streamlines are trajectories reconstructed from diffusion MRI. Tractography uncertainty remains, and the construction cohort consists of young, healthy participants. Application to other populations or acquisition pipelines requires further validation.
